# Reading the leading removal candidates

Evidence is the full before/after wikitext in [review-context.json](data/review-context.json), reproduced by `uv run labs/paragraph-survival/review.py`. The first eight distinct removing revisions were read. These are interpretations of the diff, not independent fact checks of the articles' sources. No cross-article search was performed, so transfers elsewhere cannot be ruled out.

| Rank | Article; uninterrupted days | What the removing revision actually does |
|---|---|---|

An additional set-based, case-sensitive clean-text substring check over all 30 candidates finds **4/30** complete old reading texts still present immediately after their nominal removal, and **3/30** present at the cutoff. The immediate matches are Sarka, Onni Hiltunen, Venäjän kenttälinnoitukset Suomessa ensimmäisen maailmansodan aikana, and Helsingin suurpommitukset. This is a lower bound on false semantic removals: paraphrases, facts spread over multiple blocks, changed whitespace, and transfers to other pages are not detected. See [containment.json](data/containment.json) and the flags on each retraction-index row; reproduce with `uv run labs/paragraph-survival/containment.py`.

The requested list is therefore reproducible as **slot-extinction candidates**, but cannot honestly be sold as 30 verified retractions. The data prove exact block lifetime measurement and expose a failure of the proposed semantic interpretation. Even the inferred-slot survival curve can count mergers and citation changes as deaths. Improving lineage to support many-to-one/one-to-many changes and checking clean-text containment should precede a product claim about true paragraph removal.
